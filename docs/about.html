Rent vs Buy

Make the rent-or-buy decision with your own numbers.

What it does

Rent vs Buy models your specific situation — price, rate, taxes, and how long you'll stay — into a year-by-year net-worth comparison, including what your down payment could earn if invested instead.

  • Guided setup with live address search
  • Verdict, charts, and a year-by-year schedule
  • "What if" sliders to test your assumptions
  • Save and compare scenarios
  • Private by design: no accounts, no analytics, data stays on your device

Important

For educational and illustrative purposes only. Results are estimates, not financial, tax, or legal advice.
