Frequently asked questions

Is this financial advice?
No. Rent vs Buy is an educational tool. Results are estimates based on the numbers and assumptions you enter; actual outcomes will differ. Consult a qualified professional before making a housing decision.

Where are my saved comparisons stored?
Only on your device. We have no servers and no accounts, so they are not synced between devices and can't be recovered by us if you delete the app.

How do I delete my data?
Open Settings → Clear all saved comparisons, or delete the app.

The Zillow or Redfin page won't load or doesn't show my address.
Those sites run their own bot protection and search, and sometimes block embedded browsers. Use the "open in Safari" option in the lookup sheet, or type the address on their site. You can also enter your numbers manually.

Address suggestions aren't appearing.
Suggestions come from Apple Maps and need an internet connection. You can always type the address or skip it — it's not required for the calculation.

How are the results calculated?
The app compares your net worth over time under buying versus renting and investing the difference, including mortgage, taxes, maintenance, appreciation, rent growth, and what your down payment could earn if invested. Adjust assumptions in the "What if" tab to see how sensitive the result is.
