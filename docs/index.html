Privacy Policy

Rent vs Buy for iOS · Last updated October 1, 2026

In one sentence: Rent vs Buy has no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising, and we never receive or store any of the numbers you enter — your comparisons stay on your device.

1. Information we collect

We — the developer — do not collect any information about you. Rent vs Buy has no account or sign-in, no analytics, no advertising identifiers, no crash-reporting SDKs, and no servers of our own. Home prices, rents, rates, addresses, and every other value you enter are never sent to us.

2. How your information is used

The values you enter are used only on your device to calculate the rent-versus-buy comparison, draw the charts, and populate your saved comparisons. Nothing is sold, shared for advertising, or used to build a profile of you.

3. Where your data lives

StoragePurposeLeaves your device?
On-device app storage (UserDefaults) Saved comparisons and your preferences (appearance, default assumptions) No

If you use Apple's iCloud device backup, your device's operating system may include this locally stored data in your personal iCloud backup. That backup is controlled by you and Apple, not by us.

4. Third parties & services

Rent vs Buy contains no third-party SDKs. A few optional features do involve other parties, and only when you use them:

  • Address search (Apple Maps). When you type a property address, the text you type is sent to Apple's MapKit service to provide autocomplete suggestions. This is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy; we do not receive it. The app does not request or use your device location.
  • Zillow and Redfin lookups. If you tap one of these options, the app opens that company's website in an in-app browser, with the address you entered used to search. Those sites are operated by Zillow and Redfin and are governed by their own privacy policies and terms; they may set cookies or collect data as described there. We are not affiliated with Zillow or Redfin and receive nothing from those visits.
  • Sharing a result. If you tap Share, iOS creates an image of your summary and opens the system share sheet. Where it goes is your choice; we do not receive it.

5. Data retention & deletion

Your data remains on your device until you remove it. You can delete saved comparisons one at a time, or use Settings → Clear all saved comparisons. Deleting the app permanently removes all of its data from your device. Because we never receive a copy, there is no server-side data for us to retain or delete.

6. Your rights and choices

  • Access & correction: everything you've entered is visible and editable in the app.
  • Deletion: delete individual comparisons, clear them all in Settings, or uninstall the app.
  • Control over third parties: simply don't use the address search or the Zillow/Redfin lookups; the calculator works without them.

7. Children's privacy

Rent vs Buy does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13. It is a financial planning tool, is not directed at children, and has no social features or user-generated content shared with others.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised and the new policy posted at this same URL. Since the app has no accounts, we cannot notify you directly — please check back here to review the current policy.
